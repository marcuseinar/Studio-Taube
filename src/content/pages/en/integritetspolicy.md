## What this website collects

This website does not collect any personal data. We use no tracking cookies,
and you do not need to provide any information to read the content.

## Visitor statistics

We use cookieless analytics that counts page views without identifying
individual visitors.

## Booking

When you book an appointment you leave this website, and the booking takes
place at Bokadirekt. The information you provide there is handled by Bokadirekt
and by Studio Taube as the recipient of the booking.

## Contact

If you have questions about how we handle personal data, please get in touch.
