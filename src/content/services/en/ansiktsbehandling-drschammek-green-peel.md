Dr. Schrammek Green Peel is a 100% natural, active medically-developed peeling performed in the salon. The treatment is intended to optimise the skin's functions from the inside out, for increased strength, health and elasticity.

When you come in, you choose one of these treatments together with your therapist:

**Green Peel Fresh Up, 50 minutes — 1,450 kr**
The milder variant, suitable for all skin types including sensitive skin. It gives a glow and has a firming, smoothing effect, which is why it is also called the "party peel". At the same time the treatment starts processes said to slow ageing, improve the uptake of active ingredients, and help skin with blackheads or acne. This is a treatment for sensitive, thin, ageing skin, since it strengthens the skin barrier.

**Green Peel Energy, 50 minutes — 1,650 kr**
This variant goes deeper, and suits you if you want to treat deeper wrinkles, pigmentation spots, enlarged pores and acne. You may look a little red afterwards, but by the next day the skin is usually presentable enough to go out. This peel suits someone busy who wants a highly effective treatment without limiting daily life.

### What does the treatment do?

Dr. Schrammek Green Peel is said to have a firming effect on tissue and to counter loss of elasticity and premature skin ageing. The treatment is intended to visibly improve enlarged pores, acne, scarring, uneven skin tone, pigmentation, sun-damaged skin, and dull or rough skin.

### What is the peel made of?

Dr. Schrammek Green Peel consists only of pure plants and herbs, among them bladderwrack, aloe vera and spirulina. Thanks to a unique combination of herbs, the skin is treated with highly concentrated nutrients said to have a healing and calming effect, while the herbs are also cleansing and stimulating.

### How do the herbs work?

The herbs share the skin's own pH value, unlike ordinary chemical peels, which is said to make it extra gentle: the skin is treated without straining the important skin barrier. The peel does not break the skin, but still has a deep-acting effect — like a workout for the skin. Through increased circulation and oxygen supply, cell metabolism is said to increase, cell renewal in the epidermis increases, connective-tissue production in the skin's structure is strengthened, and moisture content is balanced. From within, the herbs are said to act strongly antibacterial, anti-inflammatory, nourishing, strengthening, cleansing and protective for several days after the treatment.

### Home care products

Whichever treatment you choose, using the right products at home matters for the best result. Dr. Schrammek's products are formulated to help the treatment achieve its results while putting as little stress on the skin as possible. The products are calming, while helping the herbs stay active in the skin for longer, and add nourishing, strengthening and moisturising ingredients that also protect the skin.

Because of this, an "after treatment kit" for home use is always included with the treatment, at a cost of 750 kr.

### When should I avoid the treatment altogether?

If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or unwell.

### Wait before booking the treatment

- **One week after:** a cold sore outbreak, wax hair removal, mild acid products
- **2–4 weeks after:** antibiotics, chlorpromazine
- **4 weeks after:** cortisone ointment/cream/gel, fillers/botox
- **4–6 weeks after:** antidepressants containing St John's wort, microneedling/dermapen, cortisone injection/tablets, plasma pen, strong acid products, retinol
- **2 months after:** chemical peeling
- **2–3 months after:** laser for pigmentation or blood vessels
- **3 months after:** chemotherapy
- **4–6 months after:** blue peel, TCA, thread lift, facelift, isotretinoin, CO2 laser, laser skin resurfacing, dermabrasion, scar treatment

Ask us if you are unsure which category applies to you.
